## Plans, logs and branches

The original plans and append-only histories remain in each study directory.
Read dated statements such as “main not started” in their historical context.
The [timeline](../TIMELINE.md) connects these checkpoints to the completed runs.

`main` is the current integrated version. The `study/*` branches preserve earlier
workstreams; their names are not separate current releases. Historical branches
are retained, not deleted or rewritten. Use Git history for exact revisions and
the [lab log](../RESEARCH_LOG.md) for the reasoning behind changes.
