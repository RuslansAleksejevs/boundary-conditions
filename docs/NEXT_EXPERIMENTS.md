## Shared readiness gate

Before acquisition, record an approved official API, exact model/version,
sampling/reasoning configuration, service terms, applicable prices, token caps,
and explicit call/spending authorization. Prefer one already accessible model
for both studies; do not select a weaker model after seeing null effects.
The effort setting of the research assistants is not the effort setting of the
experimental model. No internal assistant credentials or subscription transport
serve as a substitute for an authorized experimental interface.

Use separate per-study monetary allocations whose sum fits the total approved
cap. Reserve worst-case cost before each request, including billable reasoning;
unknown charges after timeouts stay reserved. Disable implicit SDK retries.
Promotional credits are not usable until availability and conditions are verified;
never fall through to an unapproved paid balance.

Each study adds a small adapter inside its own directory. Preserve fresh role
contexts, request hashes, visible raw responses, timestamps/statuses, available
provider request IDs, usage and acquisition provenance. No hidden reasoning
traces are required. Add strict live-mode audit and freeze invariants without
weakening the offline-only checks or relabeling a test double as model evidence.
One in-flight request per study keeps independent budget enforcement simple.

## Study A: distinguish source labeling from transformation

1. Connect the live backend without changing the policy, permissive mock-action
   schema, scorer, or actual instruction privileges. Randomize and record condition
   order; reset state and context each time. Keep one saved summary per input.
2. Pilot on eight attacked and four clean inputs, using all eight conditions.
   Require at least 90% valid scheduled role responses and at least 80% correct
   clean executor cells (26/32). Disclose that these cells come from only four
   clean originals, not 32 independent tasks. Inspect payload integrity and cost.
3. Freeze; evaluate 40 new attacked inputs and 20 predetermined clean counterparts.
   Do not reuse inspected engineering cases. Keep one family outside empirical
   pilot tuning, while acknowledging that developers already know its template.
4. Primary comparisons: the identical summary as colleague versus external data,
   and summary versus verbatim extract under the colleague label. The second is a
   selection/deletion/rewriting bundle, not a pure paraphrasing effect. Generic
   warnings and specific provenance are secondary defense comparisons.
5. Report forbidden state effects, attempts, task completion, joint safe completion,
   missingness and cost. Resample whole scenario clusters with clean counterparts;
   retain unmatched-length cases. Bytes are not tokens and lexical retention is
   not semantic annotation. Report actual provider token usage separately.

Use the first eligible success/failure/clean trace in frozen order for transparent
examples, not outcome-ranked demonstrations. Audit every observed success. If
there are no successes, say so and do not substitute scripted successes.
The allowlist replay is an engineering control, not another model experiment.

## Study B: distinguish faithful translation from a convincing certificate

1. Keep the independently tested solver/checker. Add live acquisition and
   cross-bundle semantic/original-skeleton exclusions against released or inspected
   engineering fixtures and empirical development data. Record the exclusion set.
   Do not claim novel mutation families or independence from shared templates.
2. Pilot: eight natural tasks, eight injected faults and four equivalent rewrites.
   Require at least 90% valid scheduled responses and at least 10/12 clean originals
   correct in all their scheduled conditions. Use actual translator responses.
3. Freeze; run 40 new natural tasks (half SAFE/UNSAFE) through translator/checker/
   reviewer. Separately replay 40 injected faults and 20 equivalent rewrites with
   identical original/candidate/certificate under PASS, neutral, and scoped labels.
   The original remains visible to every reviewer. Randomize B/N/S order.
4. Primary: false SAFE, scoped minus PASS. Neutral minus PASS is secondary.
   Report utility, abstention, witness validity and uncertainty. Never pool the
   natural and injected populations into a spontaneous failure frequency.
5. Every real false SAFE needs the actual answer, valid local certificate and
   original counterexample confirmed by scalar and vector evaluators. Mathematical
   verification does not authenticate the provider response's provenance.

This tests certificate **presentation**, not the causal effect of having a proof
at all: no no-certificate arm is included. Zero failures is an allowed result,
not a reason to hide the original or change models. Oracle binding and textual
identity remain engineering controls; neither establishes general model safety.

## Stop, report, and integrate

Advance from pilot based on interface correctness and useful completion, not
whether attacks worked. Preserve nulls, ceilings, failed pilots and missing data.
If an access or budget prerequisite is absent, report that concrete blocker;
another synthetic rehearsal does not resolve it.

After acquisition: one primary figure, a utility/cost table, checkable examples
when observed, full denominators/intervals, limitations and a standalone English
report for each study. Reserve time for auditing and report preparation; do not
expand into another model, UI, training or additional benchmark. Changes stay in
separate study branches, with peer review and sequential integration. Competition
submission and author approvals remain separate from repository maintenance.
