\newcommand{\RunAbstract}{An exact local proof need not establish the task a user
originally requested. We examine this boundary in a finite translator--solver--reviewer
pipeline with strict Boolean contracts and independently checked exhaustive
certificates. Honest translation is separated from planted handoff faults.
For fixed original tasks, altered candidates and valid local evidence, a model
reviewer sees an ordinary PASS heading, a neutral heading or explicit candidate-only
scope wording. Every condition retains the original task and correspondence-checking
instruction. After a passing development pilot, the frozen Qwen3.5-122B-A10B
evaluation covered 100 new originals and 260 scheduled roles. All three
presentations returned correct original counterexamples on all 40 planted faults
and correct SAFE answers on all 20 equivalent controls. Primary and secondary
paired false-SAFE differences were zero on 40 complete pairs each; degenerate
bootstrap intervals do not establish equivalence, and the bounded-iid interval
was approximately [-0.4295,0.4295]. Natural translation preserved both open
contract functions in 37/37 valid translations, while three malformed translations
and one malformed review reduced useful completion to 36/40. The main required
259 physical attempts, including two identical technical retries with unknown
failed-attempt charges retained in the budget. No false guarantee was observed.
The result is a reproducible null under an explicit, small-task boundary condition,
not a general mathematical equivalence criterion, demonstrated scope-label benefit
or model-safety guarantee.}

\newcommand{\DiscussionResults}{This evaluation did not elicit the proposed
false-assurance failure on any assessed case. With an unambiguous original and
explicit correspondence instruction visible even in B, the model returned correct
original-task verdicts and witnesses despite the altered candidates' locally
valid SAFE certificates.
Scope wording showed no observed advantage; the sample and dependence assumptions
do not support a claim that wording is universally irrelevant. The actual loss
of useful completion came from strict-format failures, which are not evidence
of semantic drift. The Markdown-wrapped answer was not rescued after inspection.
The null, all malformed records and both unknown charges are retained without
changing the original sample or adding a more favorable experiment.}
\newcommand{\ConclusionResults}{The frozen real-model evaluation completed with
no observed false guarantee: all three presentations correctly handled every
planted fault and equivalent control. Natural useful completion was 36/40, with
no open-function drift among 37 valid translations. This is a transparent null
under the tested visible-original, finite-contract conditions, not a safety
guarantee, a proof of presentation equivalence or a new general criterion of
mathematical faithfulness.}
