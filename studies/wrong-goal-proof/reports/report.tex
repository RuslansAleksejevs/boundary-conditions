\section{Introduction}
A correct proof answers the statement it proves, not automatically the task a
user intended. An agent team can therefore produce locally impeccable evidence
and still answer the wrong question. The scientifically relevant issue here is
not whether a verifier can validate a finite proof, but how a downstream agent
uses that proof when the original task remains visible.

We study a deliberately small translator--solver--reviewer pipeline. The
translator and reviewer are separate model contexts; the solver and certificate
checker are local code. We separate honest translation drift from deliberately
inserted handoff faults. On fixed original tasks, candidates and valid local
certificates, we compare an ordinary PASS heading (B), a neutral heading (N), and
an explicit candidate-only scope statement (S). Our contributions are a fully
checkable finite-contract measurement setup, a paired presentation comparison,
and an auditable accounting of correctness, useful completion and uncertainty.
Neither a new general equivalence criterion nor a novel guarantee from an oracle
gate is claimed. The result-specific conclusion is stated in Section~6.

\section{Related Work}
Li et al.'s SemantEq~\cite{semanteq} explains why unrestricted bidirectional
implication can equate unrelated closed theorems: both may be independently
provable. Its proof-term policy addresses faithful formal statement comparison.
Our setting is narrower. Atom meanings and inputs are fixed, and we compare the
admissibility and obligation functions separately. This is a contract-preservation
convention, not a solution to faithful mathematical autoformalization.

Mei and Douglas~\cite{mechanical} combine semantic review with mechanical
numerical and vacuity checks for agentic autoformalization. The insufficiency of
checking only a proof is established prior work. Our comparison instead holds
the actual local evidence fixed and varies how its scope is presented to the
reviewer. We do not reproduce either paper's benchmark, quote their findings as
our measurements, or claim priority for statement/proof mismatch.

\section{Methods}
\subsection{Contracts and exact ground truth}
For four Boolean inputs $x=(p,q,r,s)$ with fixed meanings, a contract is a pair
$(C,Q)$. Its original claim is
\[
                 \forall x\in\{0,1\}^4:\ C(x)\Rightarrow Q(x).
\]
SAFE means this claim is true. UNSAFE requires a complete Boolean assignment
with $C(x)=1$ and $Q(x)=0$; UNRESOLVED is an allowed abstention. The strict JSON
AST supports constants, atoms, NOT, AND, OR and implication, with exact node
keys, bounded depth and size. Arbitrary code and \code{eval} are not used.

The original AST is rendered in an unambiguous controlled notation. A recursive
scalar evaluator and a separate integer bit-vector implementation check all 16
states. A SAFE certificate carries a complete table and the candidate AST hash;
the independent checker rejects missing/duplicate states, wrong values and wrong
hashes. UNSAFE witnesses are independently checked. These implementations share
syntax validation and hashing, so their diversity does not eliminate every
possible common-mode defect. Empty-domain/vacuity checks are unit tests, not a
main fault family.

Open-function drift means $C\not\equiv C'$ or $Q\not\equiv Q'$. We separately
record the truth of the closed original and candidate claims. Two true closed
claims can have different open functions; different syntax can preserve both.
A changed intermediate contract need not cause a wrong final answer. A malformed
translation is not counted as demonstrated semantic drift.

\subsection{Populations and paired intervention}
The preplanned evaluation has 100 new originals. Natural A uses 40 originals,
balanced 20 SAFE/20 UNSAFE, with an honest translator followed by reviewer B
(80 role calls). Controlled B uses 40 different UNSAFE originals and known altered
SAFE candidates: restricting $C$, dropping a conjunct of $Q$, replacing AND by OR
in $Q$, or reversing an implication in $Q$ (ten per family). Each altered
candidate has a nonempty admissible domain and a valid local certificate.
Twenty originally SAFE controls use syntactically altered but equivalent $C/Q$.

Each controlled or equivalent original is reviewed under B, N and S in separate
fresh contexts (180 role calls). All arms see the original task, candidate AST,
certificate and hash. The common instruction explicitly asks the reviewer to
answer the original and check correspondence. S receives no extra reference AST
or counterexample. The headings are approximately character-matched, not claimed
to be tokenizer-matched; the intervention includes semantic scope wording, not
merely typography. Case and condition orders are seeded and frozen. There is no
no-certificate arm or budget-matched single-agent comparison.

\subsection{Pilot, model and prospective controls}
The pilot schedules eight natural, eight planted and four equivalent originals:
52 role calls. At least 47/52 responses must parse and at least 10/12 clean
originals must be correct; an equivalent original must be correct in all three
arms. A null fault result does not fail this gate. Main requires a passing
audited pilot, prospective freeze and a separate source-bound authorization.

The fresh seed is 2026110816. Fourteen released/inspected engineering bundles
are excluded. Original/candidate paired C/Q truth tables and original atom-erased
AST skeletons do not overlap development and evaluation; actual pilot translations
are checked too. Higher-level mutation families remain shared, so this is not
an unseen-family benchmark. Selection uses exact mathematics, not model outcomes.

The experimental subject is \code{qwen/qwen3.5-122b-a10b} through OpenRouter's
fixed Alibaba route~\cite{qwen,openrouter}. Temperature is 0.6, top-p 0.95,
reasoning enabled and its text excluded, requested output 8192 tokens, and no
fallback provider or JSON response mode. Visible answers and usage are retained;
hidden reasoning text is not. The public model alias is not an immutable weight
pin, and provider quantization is unknown.

The study allocation is USD20. Each dispatch first reserves USD0.61341696,
covering the full advertised 262144-token context separately at input/output
tariffs USD0.26/2.08 per million. Actual total input plus output is checked against
the advertised context. Native requested output limits are not assumed to cap
all thinking tokens. Complete valid billing settles the larger of reported cost
and the no-cache token-price bound; unknown charges retain the full reserve.
The upfront-full-campaign-fit rule was prospectively relaxed, not the per-request
hard wallet. Completion is therefore not guaranteed; a budget stop leaves
scheduled cases unassessed rather than shrinking the sample.

Before this study's first request, an observed provider SSE terminal-usage footer
was accommodated and rate limits in a companion study motivated 20-second
minimum attempt-start spacing. HTTP429 waits at least 60 seconds and respects
valid Retry-After up to 300 seconds; longer values stop. Total HTTP wall time is
180 seconds, streamed bytes are capped, and waits are chunked. These operational
amendments did not change tasks, prompts, model settings or gates. At most one
identical technical retry per role and 15 globally are permitted. The complete
core schedule is 312 logical roles; blocked dependencies need no HTTP request,
whereas a technical retry adds one. A genuine interface-repair pilot, only if needed and
separately authorized, gives an absolute 379-attempt ceiling.

\subsection{Outcomes and uncertainty}
False assurance is a reviewer SAFE answer when an original counterexample exists.
Every claimed false assurance must retain the actual answer, candidate certificate
and original witness, confirmed by both evaluators. We also report correct
completion, valid UNSAFE witnesses, abstentions, malformed/missing responses and
equivalent-control utility. Natural drift and planted faults are never pooled.

The primary contrast is planted-fault false SAFE S minus B; N minus B is secondary.
Counts and marginal Wilson intervals accompany 5000 paired contract-level
bootstrap draws (seed606), complete-pair denominators, and scheduled missingness
bounds. A bounded-iid Hoeffding interval shows why a degenerate zero-discordance
bootstrap is not proof of equivalence. Shared templates limit all inferential
claims: the intervals are assumption-dependent summaries; independence across
shared-template cases and generalization to real-world tasks are not established.
The original-AST oracle gate is a privileged engineering ceiling with
direct trusted-AST access and exact computation, not an interface- or resource-
matched empirical defense or a new safety discovery.

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\DiscussionResults\par
The original task and an explicit correspondence-checking instruction are visible
even in B. This is a meaningful boundary condition, not a blind reviewer designed
to manufacture failures. The small finite language, constructive balanced task
generation, shared mutation templates, one hosted model and separate stateless
contexts limit transfer to general mathematics, long-lived swarms or production
tools. A presentation effect would not establish a causal effect of certificates
themselves because there is no no-certificate control. The fixed atom convention
does not define mathematical faithfulness beyond this testbed.

Future studies could prospectively vary task complexity, correspondence guidance,
model family and longer handoff chains while retaining exact witnesses and
information-matched controls. Those are new experiments, not reasons to alter
this frozen sample after observing a null or an error.

\section{Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations}
\textbf{Scope and external validity.} Four fixed Boolean inputs and exhaustive
ground truth make this a controlled measurement, not a claim about theorem
proving in general. The original task remains fully visible to reviewers in
unambiguous controlled notation. The oracle's privilege is direct access to the
trusted original AST and exhaustive computation, not hidden task semantics;
reviewers have no equivalent reference-AST/solver interface. Shared parsing/hashing
is a remaining common-mode trust assumption despite
the scalar/vector implementation diversity. Sampling seeds reproduce cases and
order, not the hosted model's stochastic outputs or undisclosed weight revision.
Missing outcomes remain unassessed and cannot be converted into safe decisions.

\textbf{Dual use.} Statement substitutions illustrate a false-assurance mechanism
that could be misused in larger systems. The release is confined to synthetic
finite contracts, with no external tools, real service attack, private documents,
credential access in model prompts or production exploit. Publishing exact
counterexample checks and benign-equivalence utility controls supports auditing
rather than presenting universal rejection as a useful defense.

\textbf{Data handling and resource use.} Only synthetic tasks were sent. The
selected provider policy permits logging for 30 days, reports no training on
submitted data, and does not promise zero retention. That policy was accepted
prospectively and is not described as ZDR. API credentials, arbitrary provider
error bodies, personal filesystem paths and private coordination identifiers are
not public artifacts. Reasoning text is excluded and discarded if encountered;
reasoning-token usage is retained for cost accounting. The contributor-funded
shared experimental allocation was USD50, with a hard USD20 allocation here;
no sponsor, grant, prize or automatic top-up is implied. Research-assistant
session costs are separate and not measured as part of the API result totals.

\textbf{LLM Usage Statement.} GPT-6 Astra research-assistant sessions supported
design critique, implementation, tests, experiment orchestration, analysis and
draft writing, with separate-context read-only source reviews. Qwen was the
experimental subject, not a human author. Deterministic mathematical claims are
checked by both evaluators and falsifiable certificates; model records are audited
without claiming provider-signed provenance. Human author review is pending,
so no final author-contribution or affiliation statement is invented.

\textbf{Preexisting work.} The study testbed was implemented during the November
2026 sprint, with the earlier local design recorded separately. Python's standard
library, Git, TeX tooling and the cited scientific work are preexisting resources;
their functionality and results are not claimed as new contributions. The
official report template informed this structure and was adapted as permitted.
No affiliation with or endorsement by the organizer is asserted.
