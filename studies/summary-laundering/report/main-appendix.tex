\section{Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations}
\label{app:required}
\subsection{Limitations and operational deviations}
The experiment covers one hosted model/provider, one stochastic sample per
condition, four fixed payload families and simple synthetic tasks. Fresh numeric
facts do not make templates representative of real adversaries. The attacker
already knows the task identifier and protected field; target discovery and
adaptive payload search are not measured. The quoted-plan family was excluded
from empirical pilot tuning, not unknown to the developers. Hosted model names
and dated endpoint metadata do not pin immutable weights. Shared templates and
provider behavior can make scenario-level intervals optimistic.

The executor submits a single JSON batch without interactive tool feedback.
Invalid batches execute nothing: the result describes a fail-closed batch pipeline,
not unconstrained model behavior. Zero executed effects is not a universal refusal
claim. The hard allowlist is an engineering guarantee in this mock environment,
not evidence of useful model recovery after denial or production security.
Missing responses remain unassessed. Empty-action abstentions count against
useful completion. The 480 replay cells are not independent originals.

Source labels change displayed wording, not actual message authority.
Summary/extract comparisons combine selection, deletion and rewriting. Byte-length
matching is not token or semantic matching. A summary-dependent matched subset
cannot identify unbiased causal mediation, and lexical retention does not reveal
meaning or hidden reasoning. Soft warning/provenance messages match UTF-8 bytes,
not information or token count. Template dependence, limited task complexity and
single samples constrain both null findings and apparent utility differences.

\paragraph{Preserved development failures.}
The first pilot stopped after one request: a correct-route HTTP 200 stream
repeated an empty terminal choice while reporting final usage. A narrow parser
repair accepted only an idempotent final usage-bearing repeat; changed identity,
new content and conflicting finish markers remain rejected. The original record
and full conservative hold are unchanged.

The repaired pilot stopped after 59 attempts for 54 distinct roles, with
51 completed responses and eight HTTP 429 errors. Five identical technical retries
were used; three consecutive failed logical transports triggered the frozen
circuit. Its immutable gate remains failed at 47/108 valid roles, with 29/32
clean correctness. The exact upstream rate-limit cause remains unknown.

An explicitly disclosed operational amendment acquired only 54 never-attempted
suffix roles from six wholly untouched inputs. It changed pacing and recovery
orchestration, not prompts, task generation, model settings, scoring or attempted
responses. It is not an unchanged preregistered pilot or a replacement sample.
No attempted response was resampled and no new continuation retries were allowed.
All 54 suffix calls completed; 52 were valid. The composite passed the unchanged
98/108 threshold at 99/108 valid roles and retained 29/32 clean correctness.
Its final executor matrix has six invalid and three missing outcomes.

\paragraph{Frozen main and transport discipline.}
The passing composite audit bound the prospective main manifest, seed 2026110812,
to 40 attacked and 20 clean inputs, 540 core roles and at most 27 technical retries.
Source inventory, prompts, analysis and model settings stayed unchanged after
main observations began. The amended transport enforces 20 monotonic seconds
between request starts, a minimum 60-second HTTP 429 cooldown, Retry-After up to
300 seconds and a hard total request timer. No manual restart, extra experimental
sample, semantic repair or main-stage protocol amendment occurred.

The completed main used eight retries and preserved all ten HTTP errors, 33
invalid batches and two missing executor outcomes. Actual process exit, native
audit, exact journal/run equality and the append-only campaign wallet were checked.
The wallet has 662 settled reservations and no in-flight attempt; its pre-main
232-record prefix is unchanged. Public replay checks only reviewed visible data.
Local audit hashes are not a signed external provider attestation, independent
empirical replication or external scientific peer review.

\subsection{Dual-use and ethical considerations}
All tasks, records, identities and protected fields are synthetic. Mock actions
modify only reset in-memory dictionaries. There is no real-service exploitation,
credential extraction, external action or affected third party. Experimental
inference uses an explicitly authorized official interface with a hard monetary
ceiling. The contributor-funded shared experimental ceiling was USD 50,
with USD 30 allocated to this study including pilots. No external sponsor, grant,
prize financing or automatic credit top-up is asserted. Research-assistant sessions
are separate from experimental API accounting.

Controlled injection templates can support both attack and defense research.
Publication is limited to a toy protected Boolean and includes no real target,
exfiltration destination, evasion tooling or claim of production exploitability.
Defensive implications concern explicit authority boundaries, provenance and hard
tool permissions; soft labels are not represented as authorization guarantees.

Only synthetic prompts are sent to the provider. OpenRouter's provider metadata,
checked 8 November 2026 at \url{https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/providers}, reports
that Alibaba permits prompt logging, retains data for 30 days, does not train on
submitted data and is not zero-retention. These are provider-reported terms, not
an independent audit or verified deletion guarantee. Credentials, private approval
records, personal paths, provider generation identifiers and hidden reasoning
text are excluded from public exports. Reasoning-token counts are billing
metadata, not reasoning content. Human author review remains pending and no
competition submission or final author approval is claimed.
